How to play
  • Type any 5-letter word and press Enter or Guess.
  • Each guess's SHA-1 checksum is compared to the secret word's checksum.
  • ↑ Higher — your checksum is numerically greater than the target.
  • ↓ Lower — your checksum is numerically less than the target.
  • Distance — how far apart the checksums are (0 % = exact, 100 % = opposite ends).
  • ▲ closer / ▼ farther — change vs. your previous guess.
  • Guess the exact word to win! There is no guess limit.
