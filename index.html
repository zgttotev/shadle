How to play
  • Type any 5-letter word, use the keyboard below, then press Enter or Guess.
  • Each guess's SHA-1 checksum is compared to the secret word's checksum.
  • Heat — your closeness percentile among all valid words (100 % = exact). Green is hot, yellow is warm, and red is cold.
  • Points — earn 0–100 points from heat, plus a 100-point bonus for solving.
  • ▲ hotter / ▼ cooler — heat change vs. your previous guess.
  • Guess the exact word to win! There is no guess limit.
